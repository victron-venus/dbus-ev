# Code of conduct

Participation in this project must be respectful and harassment-free, regardless of background, identity, experience or ability. Welcome newcomers, give constructive technical feedback, respect privacy, and disagree about ideas without personal attacks.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, sexualized attention, deliberate intimidation and publishing private information without permission are unacceptable in issues, pull requests, discussions and project-related communication.

Report conduct concerns privately to [alvit.work@gmail.com](mailto:alvit.work@gmail.com). The [primary maintainer](GOVERNANCE.md) receives and handles these reports. Include only information needed to explain the concern; do not publish incident details or personal information in public issues.

The maintainer may edit or remove abusive content or restrict participation proportionately, explaining decisions when privacy permits. A reporter may request reconsideration with new evidence; conflicts of interest must be disclosed.

This policy applies to maintainers as well as contributors. Its enforcement currently depends on the sole maintainer; it does not claim an independent appeals panel.
